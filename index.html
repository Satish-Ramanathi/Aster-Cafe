ABOUT US

Aster Cafe and Kitchen

Your go-to destination for delicious food, refreshing drinks, and a warm, inviting atmosphere. Whether you're stopping in for a morning coffee, a leisurely lunch, or a special dinner, we offer a diverse menu crafted with fresh, high-quality ingredients to satisfy every craving.

Our cafe and restaurant blend comfort with quality—serving everything from handcrafted espresso drinks and pastries to hearty meals and gourmet specialties. Whether you're dining in, ordering takeaway, or planning an event, our friendly team is dedicated to providing exceptional service and a memorable culinary experience.

Come relax, connect, and indulge at Aster Cafe and Kitchen where every meal is made with care, and every guest is treated like family.

OUR SERVICE

Cozy Cafe for Events, Dining & Creative Experiences

Birthday party

Make your birthday extra special at Aster! Whether it’s an intimate celebration or a lively gathering, we offer customized décor, curated menus, and a cozy ambiance that makes every birthday unforgettable.

Bridal shower

Host a dreamy bridal shower in our elegant café space. With personalized themes, floral décor, and handcrafted menus, Aster creates the perfect setting for the bride-to-be and her closest circle.

Art work shops

Fuel your creativity with our weekend art workshops. From painting and pottery to journaling and calligraphy, Aster transforms into a creative hub where you can sip, paint, and connect.

Catering & Events

Create unforgettable memories with our professional Catering & Event Services designed for all occasions. From intimate family functions to large corporate events, we deliver flawless experiences with delicious food, stylish presentation, and seamless coordination.
